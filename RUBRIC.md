# Rubric

Graded by criterion, 100 points total. Points are deducted from each criterion's maximum
using the deductions listed below.

| Criterion | Points |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Paper: Organization & Presentation | 15 |
| 2. Paper: Purpose & Contribution | 20 |
| 3. Paper: Methodology & Implementation | 20 |
| 4. Project Repository | 15 |
| 5. Video Demo | 15 |
| 6. Presentation | 15 |

## 1. Paper: Organization & Presentation (15 pts)

Full credit requires all five sections to be present and logically structured: abstract,
introduction, related work, design & implementation, and conclusion. Sections that are
missing, misordered, or broken into unnecessary subsubsections lose 3 pts.

Follow the [LNCS format](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/springer-lecture-notes-in-computer-science/kzwwpvhwnvfj):
the abstract should be 150–250 words, and keywords and authors' emails should be present.

- Abstract not 150–250 words (-1 pt)
- Emails missing (-1 pt)
- Keywords missing (-1 pt)
- No roadmap paragraph (-1 pt)
- Bullet points used instead of prose (-1 pt)
- Manuscript is not properly formatted (-1 pt)
- Non-standard outline (-1 pt)
- No figures or tables (-1 pt)
- No figures (-0.5 pts)
- No tables (-0.5 pts)
- Figures or tables not placed correctly (-0.5 pts)
- Figures not properly formatted or lacking a caption (-0.5 pts)
- Tables not properly formatted or lacking a caption (-0.5 pts)

## 2. Paper: Purpose & Contribution (20 pts)

Full credit requires that the purpose, contributions, and visualization challenges
addressed be clearly articulated. References are relevant and correctly cited in LNCS
format. Limitations and future work are thoughtfully discussed.

- Purpose is not clearly articulated (-1 pt)
- Visualization contributions are not clearly articulated (-2 pts)
- Visualization challenge(s) are not clearly articulated (-2 pts)
- References are missing or not cited in LNCS format (-1 pt)
- Limitations and future work are not thoughtfully discussed (-1 pt)

## 3. Paper: Methodology & Implementation (20 pts)

The design and implementation process is well explained and justified. Gen AI, if used,
is effectively integrated. The visual display demonstrates sound technical and design
decisions.

- The design process is not well explained and justified (-1 pt)
- The implementation process is not well explained and justified (-1 pt)
- Visual display does not demonstrate sound design decisions (-1 pt)

## 4. Project Repository (15 pts)

Uses Bootstrap and React or Vue.js effectively. The codebase is well structured.
`README.md` clearly documents setup, usage, and system description. The project is
deployed and publicly accessible.

- App is not at the top level of the repository (-1 pt)
- Bootstrap loaded from a CDN, not as a proper dependency (-0.5 pts)
- Bootstrap not used (-1 pt)
- Marginal use of Bootstrap (-0.5 pts)
- React or Vue.js is not used (-4 pts)
- `README.md` does not describe the project (-1 pt)
- `README.md` does not document setup and usage (-1 pt)
- Not deployed (-4 pts)
- Deployment is not publicly accessible (-1 pt)

## 5. Video Demo (15 pts)

Full credit requires a YouTube link in the submission, a video under 2 minutes with a
voice-over, showcasing the purpose, system contributions, functionality, and design.

- Link not provided (-10 pts)
- Video is private or unlisted and cannot be viewed (-1 pt)
- Video exceeds 2 minutes (-1 pt)
- No voice-over present (-1 pt)
- Missing purpose (-2 pts)
- Missing contributions (-2 pts)
- Missing functionality (-2 pts)
- Missing design (-2 pts)

## 6. Presentation (15 pts)

The presentation is polished and engaging. Purpose, contributions, and challenges are
clearly conveyed. It effectively incorporates video clips, explains visual design choices,
and demonstrates the work effectively. It discusses limitations and future work.
